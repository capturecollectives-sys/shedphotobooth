Common Questions

Quick
Answers.

How far in advance should I book?

As early as possible, especially for summer festivals and December events. Popular dates fill up fast. We recommend getting in touch at least 6–8 weeks before your event.

Do you travel outside Scotland?

Yes we're available UK-wide. Travel costs are factored into your quote. We've worked at events across Scotland and are happy to discuss events further afield.

Can the booth be fully branded to our event?

Absolutely that's the whole point. Every build is custom. We work with your brand assets to create graphics, signage, and photo overlays that feel like a natural part of your event.

What happens if something goes wrong on the day?

We provide 24/7 on-site operational support for every booking. Our team stays on-site throughout your event to make sure everything runs without a hitch.
