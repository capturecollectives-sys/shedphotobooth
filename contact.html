Before you write

BookingHow far ahead should we book?

As early as you can. Summer festivals and December dates go first, so six to eight weeks ahead is a good minimum.

WhereDo you work outside Scotland?

Yes. We're based in Scotland and take bookings across the UK. Travel is part of your quote.

BrandingCan the booth carry our brand?

Yes. The shed's dressing, the welcome screen, the print layout and any digital extras can all be designed around your event, sponsor or brand.

On the dayWhat if something goes wrong on the day?

We look after every booth for the whole event and watch it live from our end, so problems get fixed fast.
