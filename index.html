Photobooth Hire FAQs

Quick Answers
Before You Book.

Straight answers for event teams, couples and brands planning photobooth hire in Scotland or across the UK.

Do you offer photobooth hire outside Scotland?

Yes. Shed Photobooth is based in Scotland and available for selected events across the UK, with travel included in your tailored quote.

What events do you cover?

We cover festivals, weddings, Christmas markets, corporate events, sports events, private parties, venue activations and branded experiences.

Can the photobooth be branded?

Yes. The booth exterior, welcome screen, print layout and digital graphics can be designed around your event, sponsor or brand identity.

What is included with each booking?

Each booking includes delivery, setup, operation, instant prints, a digital gallery, custom graphics and on-site support throughout the event.
