How it works

How a session goes

  1. SHED ▸ 11A

    Step 1: Join the queue

    Guests spot the lit sign and queue up. One group goes in at a time.

  2. SHED ▸ 22A

    Step 2: Step inside

    Red velvet, a ring light, a proper camera and a touch screen.

  3. SHED ▸ 33A

    Step 3: Touch to start

    If guests pay, they tap a card on the reader. A touch on the screen starts the session.

  4. SHED ▸ 44A

    Step 4: Three, two, one

    A countdown, a photo, and again. Guests can pick a look first, from colour to black and white.

  5. SHED ▸ 55A

    Step 5: The print drops

    Moments later the print slides out, designed for your event.

  6. SHED ▸ 66A

    Step 6: Take it home

    And they walk off holding it. Where the booth offers it, they can save their photos to their phone too.

Questions

Before you book.

Still wondering? Email photo@shedphotobooth.com and we’ll reply within 24 hours.

How much does it cost?

Every booking is quoted for the event: it depends on the date, where it is, how long you need the shed and how it is dressed. Tell us a little about your event and we'll send a quote, usually within 24 hours.

How much space and power do you need?

The shed is 8 by 6 feet (about 2.4 by 1.8 metres) and needs a flat spot with a little room around the door for a queue, one standard plug socket nearby, and van access close by for set-up. It works indoors or outdoors.

Do you work outside Scotland?

Yes. We're based in Scotland and take bookings across the UK. Travel is part of your quote.

What kinds of events do you do?

Festivals, Christmas markets, sports events, weddings, corporate events, brand activations and private parties.

How many guests can it handle?

A group is in and out in a couple of minutes, so at busy times the shed handles around 40 sessions an hour. For big crowds we can bring more than one booth.

What do guests take home?

A print made on the spot on proper photo paper: a 6 by 4 inch print or a 2 by 6 inch strip, designed for your event. Where the booth offers it, guests can also save their photos and a GIF to their phone.

Does the event pay, or do the guests?

Either. The event can cover every session, or the booth runs as a paid attraction and guests pay by card at the booth.

Can the booth carry our brand?

Yes. The shed's dressing, the welcome screen, the print layout and any digital extras can all be designed around your event, sponsor or brand.

How far ahead should we book?

As early as you can. Summer festivals and December dates go first, so six to eight weeks ahead is a good minimum.

Are you insured?

Yes. We carry public liability insurance and can send our certificate and risk assessment for your event paperwork.

What if something goes wrong on the day?

We look after every booth for the whole event and watch it live from our end, so problems get fixed fast.

What happens to guests' photos?

Guests' photos are covered by our guest notice, which says what we keep and for how long: photos.shedphotobooth.com/privacy.
